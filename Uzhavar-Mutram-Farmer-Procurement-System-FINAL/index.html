❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
How do I rent stockyard storage for my harvested crops?

Go to the 'Stockyard Storage Rental' tab, select your nearest climate-controlled warehouse, enter quantity in Metric Tons and duration in days. You receive an instant digital storage receipt with live temperature monitoring.

How are crop weights standardized?

All weights are standardized in **Kilograms (Kg)** and **Metric Tons (MT)** (1 Metric Ton = 1,000 Kg). You can enter inputs in Bags, Quintals, or Metric Tons and the system calculates exact standardized equivalents automatically.

When will money reach my bank account?

Money is transferred directly into your bank account within 24 to 48 hours after crop unloading. You receive an SMS notification with the bank payment reference number.
