Cereals

6 Crops

The backbone of Indian food security. Cereal crops provide starchy grains that form the primary caloric staple for over a billion people, cultivated across diverse agro-climatic zones from Punjab's wheat plains to Tamil Nadu's paddy deltas.

📸 Category Overview — Field Stage: Wheat (rabi, harvested), Paddy Rice (flooded field), Maize (cob formation), Sorghum (grain head), Pearl Millet (spike), Finger Millet (curved grain head). AI-generated photorealistic reference images.
🟡 Harvested
Wheat
Gehu (गेहूँ) · Triticum aestivum
Golden grain heads at maturity — the characteristic bearded ear visible. Rabi (winter) crop harvested March–April.
📍Region: Punjab, Haryana, UP, MP, Rajasthan
🎯Use: Food — chapati, bread, pasta, semolina
👁️ID Mark: Long bearded golden ear; hollow straw stem
Food
🟢 Field Stage
Paddy (Rice)
Chawal / Nellikai · Oryza sativa
Flooded paddy fields with transplanted seedlings — characteristic reflective water cultivation. India's most cultivated cereal.
📍Region: West Bengal, Punjab, TN, AP, Kerala
🎯Use: Food — rice, idli, dosa, biryani, rice flour
👁️ID Mark: Long leaf blade; drooping grain panicle
Food Staple
🟡 Harvested
Maize (Corn)
Makka (मक्का) · Zea mays
Yellow grain ears harvested from tall stalks. India's 3rd most important cereal, grown in Kharif and Rabi seasons.
📍Region: Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Bihar, Rajasthan
🎯Use: Food, Feed, Starch, Biofuel, Popcorn
👁️ID Mark: Distinctive silk tassel; large yellow grain cob
Food / Feed
🌾Sorghum (Jowar)
🟢 Field Stage
Sorghum (Jowar)
Jowar (ज्वार) / Cholam · Sorghum bicolor
Compact cream-white grain panicles atop tall drought-tolerant stalks. India's 4th most cultivated cereal; a major dryland staple.
📍Region: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Rajasthan, MP
🎯Use: Food, Feed, Fodder, Brewery, Biofuel
👁️ID Mark: Compact ovoid panicle; drought-resistant thick stalk
Food / Fodder
🌾Pearl Millet (Bajra)
🟢 Field Stage
Pearl Millet (Bajra)
Bajra (बाजरा) / Kambu · Pennisetum glaucum
Cylindrical spike with tiny round grains. Most drought-tolerant cereal; critical food security crop for arid India.
📍Region: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, Maharashtra
🎯Use: Food, Feed — bhakri, roti; malt beverages
👁️ID Mark: Long cylindrical purple-brown spike (30–50 cm)
Food / Feed
🌾Finger Millet (Ragi)
🟢 Field Stage
Finger Millet (Ragi)
Ragi (रागी) / Kelvaragu · Eleusine coracana
Dark reddish-brown curved grain fingers at the spike apex. Highest calcium content of all cereals; major superfood grain.
📍Region: Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh
🎯Use: Food — mudde, ragi dosa, health foods, malt
👁️ID Mark: 4–8 curved brown finger-like grain heads at apex
Food / Health

Pulses

6 Crops

Protein powerhouses of Indian cuisine. Pulse crops fix atmospheric nitrogen, enriching soil while providing high-quality plant protein essential for vegetarian diets. India is the world's largest producer and consumer of pulses.

📸 Category Overview — Field & Harvested: Chickpea/Chana (field), Pigeon Pea/Arhar (pods), Green Gram/Moong (vine), Black Gram/Urad (dried pods), Red Lentils/Masoor (split), Kidney Beans/Rajma (pod + beans). AI-generated photorealistic reference images.
🟢 Field Stage
Chickpea (Chana)
Chana (चना) / Kadalai · Cicer arietinum
Small beige pods on bushy low-growing plants. India's #1 pulse crop by production volume; available as kabuli and desi varieties.
📍Region: MP, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, UP, Andhra Pradesh
🎯Use: Food — dal, chhole, besan, hummus, roasted snacks
👁️ID Mark: Swollen 1–2 seed pods; serrated pinnate leaves
Protein Food
🟢 Field Stage
Pigeon Pea (Arhar/Tur)
Arhar Dal / Thuvaram · Cajanus cajan
Clustered tan-green pods on tall woody perennial shrub. The base of India's beloved "dal tadka"; India produces 85% of world supply.
📍Region: Maharashtra, Karnataka, MP, Gujarat, UP
🎯Use: Food — toor dal, sambhar; nitrogen fixing cover crop
👁️ID Mark: Woody shrub; flat pods with purple tinge at maturity
Protein / Nitrogen
🟢 Field Stage
Green Gram (Moong)
Moong (मूंग) / Payatham · Vigna radiata
Slender bright-green pods clustered along trailing vine. Short-duration 60-day crop ideal for summer/rabi rotation; excellent nutritional profile.
📍Region: Rajasthan, Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka, Bihar
🎯Use: Food — dal, sprouts, khichdi, green moong curry
👁️ID Mark: Thin 7–10cm pods; bright green seed with glossy coat
Food / Sprouts
🖤Black Gram — Vigna mungo
🟡 Harvested
Black Gram (Urad)
Urad Dal (उड़द) / Ulundu · Vigna mungo
Dark black-seeded pods on slender trailing vine. Responsible for idli and dosa batter; high mucilage content aids fermentation.
📍Region: UP, MP, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Rajasthan
🎯Use: Food — urad dal, idli-dosa batter, dal makhani, papad
👁️ID Mark: Jet-black seed with white hilum; hairy cylindrical pods
Food
🟠 Processed
Red Lentil (Masoor)
Masoor (मसूर) / Mysore Paruppu · Lens culinaris
Split lentils show characteristic salmon-orange color. Fastest-cooking pulse; brown whole seeds turn orange-red when dehulled.
📍Region: UP, Bihar, West Bengal, MP, Rajasthan
🎯Use: Food — masoor dal, soup, dal fry, red lentil curry
👁️ID Mark: Lens-shaped brown pod; split seeds show orange-red
Food
🫘Kidney Bean — Phaseolus vulgaris
🟡 Harvested
Kidney Bean (Rajma)
Rajma (राजमा) / Rajma · Phaseolus vulgaris
Large dark-red kidney-shaped beans fresh from pale-green pods. A beloved North Indian dish staple; high protein and complex carbohydrate content.
📍Region: Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttarakhand
🎯Use: Food — rajma curry, salads, burritos, mixed dal
👁️ID Mark: Distinctive kidney shape; dark-red speckled seed coat
Protein Food

Oilseeds

6 Crops

India is among the world's top oilseed producers. These crops yield edible oils for cooking, industrial lubricants, biodiesel, and soaps — spanning groundnut fields of Gujarat to mustard plains of Rajasthan.

📸 Category Overview — Field Stage: Groundnut/peanut (soil-level root pods), Sunflower (yellow field), Mustard (blooming yellow field), Soybean (green pods), Sesame/Til (white pod column), Castor (spiky seed cluster). AI-generated photorealistic reference.
🟡 Harvested
Groundnut (Peanut)
Moongphali (मूंगफली) / Nilakadalai · Arachis hypogaea
Uniquely geocarpic — flowers above ground, pods develop underground. Wrinkled tan pod with 2–3 seeds harvested by uprooting entire plant.
📍Region: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh
🎯Use: Edible oil, peanut butter, roasted snacks, cattle feed
👁️ID Mark: Underground pod with net-like reticulate surface
Oil / Food
🟢 Field Stage
Sunflower
Surajmukhi (सूरजमुखी) · Helianthus annuus
Large 20–30cm circular flower head packed with black-white seeds following the sun (heliotropism). One of the cleanest edible oils.
📍Region: Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha
🎯Use: Edible oil, confectionery seeds, bird seed, biodiesel
👁️ID Mark: Large circular yellow-ray floret head; rough stem
Oil
🟢 Field Stage
Mustard (Sarson)
Sarson / Rai (सरसों) · Brassica juncea
Iconic bright-yellow flowering fields — the classic Indian winter landscape. Slender siliqua pods with tiny round seeds rich in pungent oil.
📍Region: Rajasthan, Haryana, UP, MP, West Bengal
🎯Use: Mustard oil (cooking), seeds (spice), greens (saag)
👁️ID Mark: Four-petal cross-shaped yellow flowers; slender siliqua
Oil / Spice
🟢 Field Stage
Soybean
Soyabean (सोयाबीन) · Glycine max
Hairy tri-foliate leaves with fuzzy ovoid pods. World's richest protein legume; India's crop transformed Maharashtra's Vidarbha economy.
📍Region: Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Karnataka
🎯Use: Soy oil, tofu, soymilk, animal feed, biodiesel
👁️ID Mark: Densely hairy stems and pods; tri-foliate leaf
Oil / Protein
🌿Sesame — Sesamum indicum
🟢 Field Stage
Sesame (Til)
Til (तिल) / Ellu · Sesamum indicum
World's oldest oilseed. Rectangular white pod capsules arranged vertically along stem burst open when ripe — origin of "Open Sesame!"
📍Region: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, UP
🎯Use: Sesame oil, tahini, til ladoo, bread topping, cosmetics
👁️ID Mark: Rectangular 4-valved capsules; white/black seed varieties
Oil / Food
🌿Castor — Ricinus communis
🟢 Field Stage
Castor
Arand (अरंड) / Amanakku · Ricinus communis
Spiky reddish-green seed clusters on tall ornamental plants. India supplies 80%+ of global castor oil — essential for industrial lubrication.
📍Region: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh (India = #1 globally)
🎯Use: Industrial oil, biodiesel, cosmetics, plastics, Nylon-11
👁️ID Mark: Large palmate leaves; spiny reddish-brown capsule clusters
Industrial Oil

Spices

8 Crops

India — the "Spice Capital of the World" — produces 75% of global spice varieties. These crops define Indian cuisine's aroma, color, and medicinal character, from Kerala's black pepper to Erode's turmeric.

📸 Category Overview — Harvested & Processed: Turmeric (sliced rhizome, orange-yellow), Red Chili (ripe on plant), Coriander (fresh herb + seeds), Cumin/Jeera (dried seeds), Black Pepper (vine with clusters), Ginger (root cut to reveal yellow flesh). AI-generated photorealistic reference.
🟡 Harvested
Turmeric
Haldi (हल्दी) / Manjal · Curcuma longa
Finger-like rhizomes with rough brown skin concealing vivid orange-yellow flesh. Curcumin provides anti-inflammatory & antimicrobial properties.
📍Region: Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu (Erode = "Turmeric City"), Odisha
🎯Use: Spice, medicine, dyeing, cosmetics, curry powder
👁️ID Mark: Intensely orange-yellow flesh; earthy pungent aroma
Spice / Medicine
🟢 Field Stage
Chili Pepper
Mirchi (मिर्ची) / Milagai · Capsicum annuum
Vivid red fruits ripening from green on bushy plants. Capsaicin creates the heat. India exports 1M+ tonnes annually; Guntur is world's largest chili market.
📍Region: Andhra Pradesh (Guntur), Rajasthan, Karnataka, MP
🎯Use: Fresh vegetable, dried spice, paprika, hot sauce, pickle
👁️ID Mark: Elongated fruit turning red; white pith with capsaicin glands
Spice / Vegetable
🟢 Field Stage
Coriander (Dhania)
Dhania (धनिया) / Kothamalli · Coriandrum sativum
Bipinnate aromatic leaves with small white umbel flowers producing round aromatic seeds. Dual-use crop — fresh herb leaves and dried seed spice both valued.
📍Region: Rajasthan, MP, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu
🎯Use: Fresh garnish, seed spice, essential oil, medicinal
👁️ID Mark: Rounded ribbed seed; lacy upper leaves vs lobed lower
Spice / Herb
🟠 Processed
Cumin (Jeera)
Jeera (जीरा) / Jeeragam · Cuminum cyminum
Tiny boat-shaped ridged seeds with an intensely warm, earthy aroma. India produces 70% of world supply; Gujarat's Unjha is the global cumin trading hub.
📍Region: Rajasthan, Gujarat (India = 70% of world production)
🎯Use: Tempering/tadka, spice blends, cumin water, digestive aid
👁️ID Mark: 5–6 ridges on elongated brown boat-shaped seed
Spice
🟢 Field Stage
Black Pepper
Kali Mirch (काली मिर्च) / Milagu · Piper nigrum
Woody climbing vine with pendant green-to-red peppercorn spikes. The "King of Spices" — drove ancient trade routes; Kerala's Wayanad is the heartland.
📍Region: Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu (Malabar Coast)
🎯Use: Spice (black/white/green), essential oil, piperine supplement
👁️ID Mark: 10–15cm pendant spike; round drupe green→red→black
Spice / Export
🟡 Harvested
Ginger
Adrak (अदरक) / Inji · Zingiber officinale
Knobbly beige rhizomes with pale-yellow flesh and pungent gingerol. Subterranean rhizome harvested fresh or dried; ubiquitous in chai and ayurveda.
📍Region: Kerala, Karnataka, Assam, Meghalaya, Andhra Pradesh
🎯Use: Spice, ginger tea/chai, candy, essential oil, medicine
👁️ID Mark: Gnarled knobby rhizome; fibrous pale-yellow interior
Spice / Medicine
🥦

Vegetables

6 Crops

India is the world's 2nd largest vegetable producer. These horticulture crops provide essential vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber — grown in kitchen gardens, terrace farms, and large commercial fields year-round.

📸 Category Overview — Harvested & Field Stage: Tomato (vine-ripened), Onion (fresh harvest), Potato (dug with soil), Brinjal/Eggplant (on plant), Okra/Bhindi (plant with flower), Cauliflower (large head). AI-generated photorealistic reference.
🟡 Harvested
Tomato
Tamatar (टमाटर) / Thakkali · Solanum lycopersicum
Smooth red-skinned berries in trusses. The most widely consumed vegetable globally; rich in lycopene antioxidant. Technically a fruit botanically.
📍Region: Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Odisha, UP
🎯Use: Cooking, sauces, salads, puree, ketchup, juicing
👁️ID Mark: 5-lobed calyx star; red when ripe; watery locules inside
Vegetable / Food
🟡 Harvested
Onion
Pyaz (प्याज) / Vengayam · Allium cepa
Layered spherical bulbs with papery copper-red or white skin. A kitchen essential whose price fluctuations impact Indian politics — critical commercial crop.
📍Region: Maharashtra (Nashik), Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, Gujarat
🎯Use: Cooking base, salads, pickles, dehydrated onion export
👁️ID Mark: Hollow cylindrical leaves; fleshy concentric bulb layers
Vegetable
🟡 Harvested
Potato
Aloo (आलू) / Urulaikizhangu · Solanum tuberosum
Starchy underground tubers with eyes. India's most consumed vegetable; subterranean stem tubers identified by irregular shape and shallow buds.
📍Region: Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Gujarat, MP
🎯Use: Cooking (all forms), chips, starch, alcohol, livestock feed
👁️ID Mark: Irregular oval tuber; shallow "eye" axillary buds
Staple Vegetable
🟢 Field Stage
Brinjal (Eggplant)
Baingan (बैंगन) / Kathirikkai · Solanum melongena
Glossy deep-purple ovoid fruits on bushy thorny plants. Varieties range from tiny round green to large white elongated — baigan bharta, bharwan baingan.
📍Region: Bihar, Karnataka, Odisha, Maharashtra, West Bengal
🎯Use: Curries, grilled, stuffed, bharta, sambhar, tempura
👁️ID Mark: Calyx with star pattern; glossy deep-purple skin
Vegetable
🟢 Field Stage
Okra (Bhindi)
Bhindi (भिण्डी) / Vendaikkai · Abelmoschus esculentus
Ridged green finger-pods with characteristic mucilaginous texture. Hibiscus family — beautiful yellow flowers precede fruit; harvest at 5–7 day intervals.
📍Region: Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Gujarat, Karnataka
🎯Use: Curries, stir-fry, pickling, stuffed okra, export (fresh/frozen)
👁️ID Mark: 5-ridged green finger pod; large yellow hibiscus flower
Vegetable
🟡 Harvested
Cauliflower
Phool Gobhi (फूल गोभी) / Kovippu · Brassica oleracea var. botrytis
Dense white "curd" of undeveloped flower tissue wrapped in protective green leaves. Crisp textured head; purple and orange varieties becoming popular.
📍Region: West Bengal, Bihar, Maharashtra, Odisha, Gujarat
🎯Use: Cooking, aloo-gobhi, pickles, pizza crust substitute
👁️ID Mark: Tight white dome (curd); large blue-green outer leaves
Vegetable

Fruits

6 Crops

India's tropical climate nurtures the world's richest diversity of fruits. From the royal mango to the humble guava, these crops represent horticulture wealth and export value, supporting millions of orchard farming families.

📸 Category Overview — Harvested Form: Mango (golden-orange flesh revealed), Banana (yellow bunch on plant), Papaya (halved with black seeds), Guava (cut revealing white flesh), Coconut (opened with water), Pomegranate (split showing red arils). AI-generated photorealistic reference.
🟡 Harvested
Mango
Aam (आम) / Maambazham · Mangifera indica
The "King of Fruits" — golden-orange flesh of the Alphonso variety. India produces 40%+ of world's mangoes; 1,500+ named varieties from Alphonso to Dasheri.
📍Region: UP, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Bihar, Gujarat, TN
🎯Use: Fresh eat, juice, pickle, amchur (dry), chutney, pulp export
👁️ID Mark: Single flat seed; yellow-orange sweet aromatic flesh
Fruit / Export
🟢 Field Stage
Banana
Kela (केला) / Vazhai · Musa acuminata/balbisiana
Pendant bunches of curved elongated fruits on the pseudostem. Technically a herb, not a tree. India is the world's #1 banana producer.
📍Region: Tamil Nadu (Trichy), AP, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat
🎯Use: Fresh, cooked (green), chips, banana bread, export
👁️ID Mark: Crescent-shaped fruit; purplish-red pendant flower head
Fruit
🟡 Harvested
Papaya
Papita (पपीता) / Pappali · Carica papaya
Orange-fleshed tropical fruit with central cavity of black seeds. Rich in papain enzyme used for meat tenderizing; grows on single trunk palm-like plant.
📍Region: Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Odisha
🎯Use: Fresh, juice, salad (green), papain enzyme extraction
👁️ID Mark: Central seed cavity; bright orange flesh when ripe
Fruit / Enzyme
🟡 Harvested
Guava
Amrood (अमरूद) / Koyya · Psidium guajava
Round-oval fruits with thin yellow-green skin and white-pink firm flesh. Exceptionally high in Vitamin C — 4× more than oranges; the "poor man's apple."
📍Region: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, AP
🎯Use: Fresh, juice, jam, jelly, guava paste, dried snacks
👁️ID Mark: Thin edible skin; white-pink grainy flesh with hard seeds
Fruit
🟢 Field Stage
Coconut
Nariyal (नारियल) / Thengai · Cocos nucifera
The "Tree of Life" — every part is economically useful. Green drupes at crown of 30m palms; white endosperm (copra) yields high-value cooking oil.
📍Region: Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Goa
🎯Use: Oil, coconut water, milk, copra, coir fibre, toddy
👁️ID Mark: Fibrous brown husk; 3 eyes at base of inner shell
Multi-Use
🟡 Harvested
Pomegranate
Anar (अनार) / Mathulam · Punica granatum
Leathery red-hued berry containing 600+ jewel-like ruby arils. Bhagwa and Mridula varieties dominate Indian markets; Maharashtra's Solapur is the pomegranate capital.
📍Region: Maharashtra (Solapur), Karnataka, Gujarat, Rajasthan, AP
🎯Use: Fresh arils, juice, anardana (dried), wine, medicine
👁️ID Mark: Crown (calyx remnant) at apex; hundreds of red arils
Fruit / Health

📸 Category Overview — Field Stage: Sugarcane (dense green stalks), Cotton (open white bolls), Tea (hillside plantation rows), Coffee (red cherry-bearing branches), Jute (fibrous stalks), Rubber (latex collection). Verified Unsplash stock photography.
🟢 Field Stage
Sugarcane
Ganna (गन्ना) / Karumbu · Saccharum officinarum
Towering 3–5m dense stalks with jointed nodes and high sucrose content. India is the world's #1 producer of sugarcane; supports 50M farmer families.
📍Region: UP, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat
🎯Use: Sugar, jaggery (gur), ethanol, molasses, bagasse (paper)
👁️ID Mark: Bamboo-like jointed green-purple stalks; waxy outer coating
Sugar / Fuel
🟢 Field Stage
Cotton
Kapas (कपास) / Parutti · Gossypium hirsutum / G. arboreum
Fluffy white cotton bolls bursting open from dried seed pods. India is the world's largest cotton producer; the "white gold" of Vidarbha and Gujarat.
📍Region: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, AP, MP, Punjab
🎯Use: Fiber (textile), cottonseed oil, linters, animal feed
👁️ID Mark: Fluffy white 3–5 lobed boll; okra-like green capsule before opening
Fiber / Oil
🟢 Field Stage
Tea
Chai (चाय) / Theyilai · Camellia sinensis
Manicured rows of compact shrubs on misty hillside estates. Only the "two leaves and a bud" are plucked — entirely by hand; Assam and Darjeeling are world-famous origins.
📍Region: Assam, West Bengal (Darjeeling), Tamil Nadu (Nilgiris), Kerala
🎯Use: Beverage, chai, antioxidant supplements, tea extract
👁️ID Mark: Glossy elliptical leaf with serrated margins; compact mound shape
Beverage / Export
🟠 Processed
Coffee
Kapi / Coffee · Coffea arabica / C. canephora
Deep-red coffee cherries cluster on branches under forest canopy shade. India produces shade-grown arabica and robusta; Coorg and Chikmagalur are legendary origins.
📍Region: Karnataka (Coorg), Kerala, Tamil Nadu (Nilgiris)
🎯Use: Beverage, espresso, filter coffee, export (green beans)
👁️ID Mark: Glossy paired drupes (cherries) turning red at maturity
Beverage / Export
🟢 Field Stage
Jute
Paat (पाट) / Salpa · Corchorus capsularis / olitorius
Tall (3–4m) fibrous stalks harvested by retting (water soaking) to extract strong bast fibers. The "Golden Fiber" — biodegradable packaging alternative to plastic.
📍Region: West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, Odisha (Bengal delta = ideal)
🎯Use: Gunny sacks, rope, carpet backing, geo-textile, handicrafts
👁️ID Mark: Tall unbranched stem; soft silky luster after retting
Fiber
🟢 Field Stage
Rubber
Rezan / Rubber · Hevea brasiliensis
White milky latex oozes from diagonal bark incisions into collection cups. Kerala's rubber plantations produce 90%+ of India's natural rubber for tyre manufacturing.
📍Region: Kerala (90%+), Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Assam, Tripura
🎯Use: Tyres, gloves, surgical products, elastic, industrial parts
👁️ID Mark: Trifoliate leaves; diagonal bark tapping marks; white latex
Industrial

📸 Category Overview — Harvested & Field Stage: Tapioca (root tubers), Cardamom (green pods), Cashew (apple & nut), Arecanut (palm cluster), Garlic (white bulbs), Saffron (purple crocus flowers). Verified Unsplash stock photography.
🟡 Harvested
Tapioca (Cassava)
Kappa / Maravalli · Manihot esculenta
Large starchy brown-skinned tubers with white interior harvested 10–12 months after planting. Kerala's "poor man's crop" — central to tribal food security.
📍Region: Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra
🎯Use: Food (boiled/chips), starch, animal feed, biofuel, sabudana
👁️ID Mark: Brown scaly bark; white starchy flesh; palmate leaves
Starch / Food
🟢 Field Stage
Cardamom (Elaichi)
Elaichi (इलाइची) / Elakkai · Elettaria cardamomum
Pale green tri-celled pods clustered near the base of tall ginger-family plants. The "Queen of Spices" — world's 3rd most expensive spice after saffron and vanilla.
📍Region: Kerala (Idukki), Karnataka, Tamil Nadu (Cardamom Hills)
🎯Use: Spice, chai masala, desserts, mouth freshener, medicine
👁️ID Mark: 3-ridged pale-green pod; 3 seed columns with dark aromatic seeds
Spice / Export
🟡 Harvested
Cashew
Kaju (काजू) / Mundhiri · Anacardium occidentale
Kidney-shaped nut hangs externally below fleshy yellow-red cashew apple. India is the world's #1 cashew processor; Goa and Kerala export premium grades.
📍Region: Maharashtra (Konkan), Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, AP
🎯Use: Nut (snack, cooking), cashew apple juice, CNSL (industrial oil)
👁️ID Mark: Gray-green kidney nut below fleshy red-yellow accessory fruit
Nut / Export
🟢 Field Stage
Arecanut (Betel Nut)
Supari (सुपारी) / Paakku · Areca catechu
Oval orange-green drupes in compact clusters on slender palms. Seeds dried and sliced as "supari" for traditional pan-chewing; major income crop of coastal Karnataka.
📍Region: Karnataka (Shimoga-Mangalore), Kerala, Assam, AP
🎯Use: Pan/paan chewing, ceremonial use, tanning agent, dye
👁️ID Mark: Slender graceful palm; compact clusters of smooth orange nuts

Garlic
Lahsun (लहसुन) / Poondu · Allium sativum
Compound bulb of 10–16 cloves encased in papery white skin. Allicin provides characteristic pungent aroma; dual-use as culinary spice and medicinal herb.
📍Region: Madhya Pradesh (Mandsaur), Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra
🎯Use: Cooking spice, garlic paste, dehydrated flakes, medicine
👁️ID Mark: Compound bulb; papery white skin; flat-sided individual cloves

Saffron (Kesar)
Kesar (केसर) / Kungumapoo · Crocus sativus
Each purple crocus flower yields only 3 vivid red stigma threads. World's most expensive spice by weight — hand-harvested at dawn; Kashmir's Pampore valley is the sole Indian source.
📍Region: Jammu & Kashmir (Pampore — only saffron district in India)
🎯Use: Flavor/color for biryani, sweets, saffron milk, medicine, cosmetics
👁️ID Mark: 3 bright crimson stigmas; purple flower; grass-like leaves
